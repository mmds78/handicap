WHS Handicap Calculator

Select the course, tee, enter your adjusted gross score, and add the round. For custom courses, select "Other," enter course name, Course Rating, and Slope, then add the round to save the course for future use. The app calculates score differentials and your Handicap Index based on the best 8 of your last 20 rounds (or fewer, per WHS rules).

Assumptions: Score entered is the adjusted gross score. PCC is 0. No exceptional score adjustments or caps implemented.
